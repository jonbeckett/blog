Somehow – in-between working all day – I managed to get four loads through the washing machine and tumble dryer. I also emptied the dishwasher, re-filled the dishwasher, and emptied it again. And put the rubbish out, and collected the empty bins back in again. I never really “stop”.

And then my other half’s car broke down – which meant I also cooked dinner for everybody, and washed up afterwards.

Somehow I then found time to record some content for YouTube – which I’m only too aware was “mailed in” – before answering emails, and finally sitting down in the living room – at 11pm.

It’s now heading towards 1am, and I’m looking sideways at a glass of wine. A second glass of wine. To take the edge off the day.

It’s funny really – although I like a glass of wine, I rarely drink it any more. Invariably – while at home – I will drink coffee, because I don’t want to waste the time that wine would invariably cause. I want to have my wits about me, and I don’t want a headache in the morning.

Anyway.

Like I said. It’s racing towards 1am. Time to head to bed, and fall asleep with my nose in a book – not for the first time this week.
