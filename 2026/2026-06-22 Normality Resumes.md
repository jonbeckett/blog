It’s been a week since I returned from the long weekend visiting America. Quite predictably after sitting next to a horrific little man on the flight home that coughed and sneezed without covering his mouth for eight hours, I got sick - which wiped out the latter part of the week and much of last weekend for me.

Whenever I get sick these days I remember the lung infection I had a couple of years ago - mostly caused by never slowing down. I still remember the local doctor prescribing me colossal numbers of tablets - steroids among them - to take immediately, and every few hours. They did the trick.

It’s been weird - slotting back into the daily work/life balance grind after experiencing a possible “sliding door” future. While at the FlightSimExpo several people asked if I might “make the leap” at some point - go full time with what has until now been a hobby. There was a part of me this morning - in the moments before powering up my work laptop - where I began wondering “what if?”.

You know the funny thing? Flying pretend aeroplanes on YouTube wouldn’t have been my first choice as an alternative career.

I’ve always loved writing.

In a perfect world people I would be independently wealthy and travel the world writing reflections on days filled with wonder and discovery. Of course I’m *not* independently wealthy, and realistic enough to know that writing would never pay the bills. It’s a nice daydream though.

Anyway.

After work today - in the hour before my other half got home from work - I cut the grass in the back garden. This was of course a stupid idea, given the sun’s attempts to incinerate us all at the moment, but needed given that the green bin will be collected tomorrow.

I learned an important lesson about cutting the grass this week - if your teenage daughter volunteers to cut the grass, you need to put time aside to cut it again a few days later - while she’s not around, so it doesn’t hurt her feelings.

Seriously. It looked like a deranged spider had accidentally started the lawn mower.

While mowing the grass I had some ear pods in - listening to music. It occurred to me towards the end - dripping in sweat - that there’s probably an amount of sweat that would complete a circuit. I wonder how many volts a pair of Apple AirPods can empty into your head? I wonder if they test that sort of thing?

My other half arrived home just as I finished - sitting in the newly billiard-tabled garden with a pint of water. She brought news of our younger daughters, who have travelled down to the coast for a few days - taking the youngest member of the household for her first holiday.

One thing is for sure - the house is *very* quiet without them here. I’m trying to enjoy it, but it feels odd.
